# Town Shorts Uploader

Town Shorts Uploader is a personal tool. It creates short animated "In this town" story videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to other users.

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

What we access: only the operator's own accounts, with the permissions the operator grants (uploading videos and reading basic profile information needed to upload).

What we store: access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. No data is stored on any server.

Sharing: we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Other people's data: the tool does not collect data about viewers or other users.

Deletion: access can be revoked at any time in the settings of YouTube, TikTok or Instagram. Local tokens are deleted by removing the tool's tokens folder.

## Terms of Service

The tool is used only by its operator to publish the operator's own original content.

All content follows the rules of YouTube, TikTok and Instagram and is labelled as AI-generated where required.

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty.
